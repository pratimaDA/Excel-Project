# Excel Analytics Reports

## Dataset Used for Analysis

**4 CSV files** containing mocked data of **AtliQ Technology** used for learning purposes in the **Data Analytics Bootcamp** provided by **Codebasics**.

### Datasets

* **fact_sales_monthly** (`date`, `product_code`, `customer_code`, `qty`, `net_sales_amount`)
* **dim_customer** (`customer_code`, `customer_name`, `market`, `platform`, `channel`)
* **dim_market** (`market`, `sub_zone`, `region`)
* **dim_product** (`product_code`, `product_name`, `division`, `segment`, `category`, `variant`)

---

## **1. Customer Net Sales Performance Report**

**Objective:**

To find the total sales generated by all customers in each country. A filter was provided to view total sales based on the selected region or market.

**Tech Skills Learned:**

* ETL
* Use of `SUM()` formula
* Pivot Tables
* Conditional Formatting
* Data Bars for visual representation

**Analytical Skills Learned:**

* Understanding the background calculations required to answer business questions such as:

  * Which country should we focus on to expand our business?
  * Which country is performing the best in terms of sales?

**Dataset Used:**

* `fact_sales_monthly`
* `dim_market`

---

## **2. Market Performance Target Report**

**Objective:**

To analyze the performance of each market for the years **2019, 2020, and 2021** and compare it against the given sales targets.

**Tech Skills Learned:**

* ETL
* `SUMIFS()`
* Pivot Tables
* Conditional Formatting Rules

**Analytical Skills Learned:**

* Learning how to evaluate market performance by calculating yearly sales and comparing them with predefined targets.

**Dataset Used:**

* `fact_sales_monthly`
* `dim_market`

## **3. Profit & Loss (P&L) Report**

**Objective:**

To calculate and analyze key business KPIs used in a Profit & Loss report.

**Tech Skills Learned:**

* ETL
* `SUM()`
* `SUMIFS()`
* `IF()`
* `IFERROR()`
* Create a Date Table using DAX
* Pivot Tables
* Conditional Formatting Rules

**Analytical Skills Learned:**

1. Identifying the key business KPIs required in a P&L report.
2. Understanding what each KPI indicates about business performance. For example, a higher **COGS** value may indicate opportunities to optimize operational processes and reduce costs.

**Dataset Used:**

* `fact_sales_monthly`
* `dim_market`
